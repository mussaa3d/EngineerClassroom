## Example: CHE 409 Separation Processes (graph mode)
- Data first with the homework's symbols (x_F, x_D, x_W, F, D, W, q, R); one KaTeX line per equation, the
  substitution on the next line; readings "from graph: y' = 0.77" checked by calculation; answers boxed.
- Graphs on the homework's paper and at its scale: McCabe–Thiele A4 portrait, Ponchon–Savarit A3 portrait, miscible
  extraction A3 landscape (triangle left, distribution curve right), immiscible extraction A4 landscape; x–y plots
  10 cm × 10 cm; multicomponent on plain A4 with correlation charts as pictures.
- Trial and error written as trials (guess → check → next guess, with how the next guess is chosen).

## Example: CHE 415 Process Control (whiteboard mode)
- Restate data, list ASSUMPTIONS, sketch the unit. Each balance under its own heading, starting from
  "Accumulation = In − Out ± Generation", then substitution (m = ρV, H = ρVCp(T − T_ref)), expand d(VC)/dt and cancel.
- Steady-state equations numbered (1)(2)(3); final equations boxed; units carried.
- Model questions end with Parameters / Inputs / States / DOF; dynamics questions end with order / gain / τ.
- Laplace: transform term by term, Y(s), partial fractions by the cover-up method one coefficient per line, invert.
- No graph paper: responses, pole maps, block diagrams and P&IDs drawn as plots.
